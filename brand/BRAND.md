# DeFaria & Stein brand (work in progress)

Sean is still working on this.

## Approved logos
- **Cipher:** interlocked D and S inside one thin ring. Main logo. `logos/cipher-royal.svg`, `logos/cipher-black.svg`.
- **D&S:** Times New Roman D and S with a large overlapping italic ampersand. `logos/ds.svg`. The file is live type, not outlined yet.

## Name
"DeFaria & Stein" entirely in Copperplate, tracked about .14em, including the ampersand, in black.

## Color
| Use | Hex |
|---|---|
| Background | #FFFFFF |
| Text | #000000 |
| Accent, globe lines, links | #002FA7 |

## Look
White page, one object, quiet motion. Clean thumbnails on a fine-line globe. Video plays only on hover or hold.

## Ruled out
Stripes, flannel or plaid, iPhone frames around clips, circled D&S marks, over-animated effects.

## Instagram profile picture (2026-11-09)
- **Chosen:** `instagram-profile/CHOSEN-royal-white.png`, the original Cipher unchanged, white on royal #002FA7, 1080×1080.
- Keep the original drawing, forks included. Thicker strokes and the plain D and S without the forked ends were tried and set aside.

- Explorations are kept for reference: `thick`, `ring`, `no-forks`, `crest`, `distinct`, and `materials` (embroidered flannel, letterpress, leather, wax seal, generated textures rather than photos).
